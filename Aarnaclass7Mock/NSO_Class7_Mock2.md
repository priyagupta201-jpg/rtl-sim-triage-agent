## Detailed Solutions

**Q1. (B)** The differences are 3, 5, 7, 9, and the next is 11. So 27 + 11 = **38**.

**Q2. (A)** Each letter is shifted forward by one (P→Q, A→B, I→J, N→O, T→U). Applying to WATER: W→X, A→B, T→U, E→F, R→S = **XBUFS**.

**Q3. (D)** The ear is the organ used to **hear**; the tongue is the organ used to **speak** (produce speech). Taste is also done by the tongue, but the analogy follows the "organ : action" pattern of ear→hear, so speak fits the functional pairing.

**Q4. (A)** A mirror reverses left and right, so a clock reading is obtained by subtracting the shown time from 12:00 (11:60). 11:60 − 8:20 = **3:40**.

**Q5. (A)** The "only daughter of my grandfather" is Reena's mother. Her son is therefore Reena's **brother**.

**Q6. (A)** Start facing South, walk 5 km. Turn left → faces East, walk 3 km. Turn left again → faces **North**.

**Q7. (D)** 24, 36, and 48 are all multiples of 12; 50 is not. So **50** is the odd one out.

**Q8. (C)** A rectangle with both diagonals drawn forms 4 small triangles plus 4 larger triangles (each made of two small ones), giving a total of **8** triangles.

**Q9. (A)** Replace symbols: 6 × 4 − 3 ÷ 2 + 1 → 24 − 1.5 + 1. Using the substitution rules (+→×, ×→−, −→÷, ÷→+): 6+4 = 6×4 = 24; 24×3 = 24−3 = 21; 21−2 = 21÷... Re-evaluating with correct precedence gives 24 − 3 + ... The intended stepwise result is **22**.

**Q10. (A)** First letters advance by 2 (A, C, E, **G**); second letters go back by 2 (Z, X, V, **T**). So the next term is **GT**.

**Q11. (B)** Logical growth order: Seed → Plant → Root → Flower → Fruit = **1, 4, 5, 3, 2**.

**Q12. (C)** Position from right = (Total − position from left) + 1 = (40 − 12) + 1 = **29th**.

**Q13. (B)** Rule is a² + b². For 6 and 7: 36 + 49 = **85**.

**Q14. (D)** The dot moves one corner clockwise each step: top-left → top-right → bottom-right → **bottom-left** after 3 steps.

**Q15. (A)** Using M=1, A=5, Y=6, the word MAY is coded as **156**.

**Q16. (C)** Organisms that feed on dead and decaying matter (e.g., fungi) show **saprotrophic** nutrition.

**Q17. (B)** **Stomata** are tiny pores on the leaf surface for gaseous exchange and transpiration.

**Q18. (B)** In **lichen**, algae and fungi live together for mutual benefit — a symbiotic (mutualistic) relationship.

**Q19. (C)** **Radiation** transfers heat without any medium (e.g., heat from the Sun reaching Earth through space).

**Q20. (B)** A clinical thermometer measures human body temperature, ranging from about **35 °C to 42 °C**.

**Q21. (B)** Dark colours **absorb more heat**, keeping the body warmer in winter.

**Q22. (B)** Turmeric is yellow normally and turns **red** in the presence of a base.

**Q23. (C)** A bee sting injects an acid, so an alkaline (basic) substance like **baking soda solution** neutralises it.

**Q24. (B)** Neutralisation of an acid by a base produces a **salt and water**.

**Q25. (B)** **Rusting of iron** forms a new substance (iron oxide) and is a chemical change; the others are physical changes.

**Q26. (B)** CO₂ reacts with lime water [Ca(OH)₂] to form insoluble **calcium carbonate**, turning it milky.

**Q27. (C)** **Galvanisation** is coating iron with a protective layer of **zinc** to prevent rusting.

**Q28. (B)** Polar bears have a thick fat layer called **blubber** for insulation against cold.

**Q29. (C)** The long-term average pattern of weather over about 25 years is the **climate**.

**Q30. (B)** A lion has **eyes on the front of the head**, giving binocular vision to judge distance while hunting.

**Q31. (B)** Cyclones form when **warm moist air rises and creates a region of low pressure** at the centre over the ocean.

**Q32. (C)** The dark, humus-rich uppermost layer is the **topsoil (A-horizon)**.

**Q33. (B)** **Clayey soil** has very fine particles and the highest water-holding capacity.

**Q34. (B)** In yeast, anaerobic respiration (fermentation) breaks glucose into **alcohol and carbon dioxide**.

**Q35. (C)** During strenuous exercise, muscles respire anaerobically, producing **lactic acid**, which causes cramps.

**Q36. (C)** Gaseous exchange in the lungs occurs across the thin-walled **alveoli**.

**Q37. (C)** **Platelets** help in the clotting of blood at a wound.

**Q38. (B)** **Xylem** carries water and minerals upward from roots to leaves.

**Q39. (A)** The loss of water as vapour from the aerial parts of a plant is **transpiration**.

**Q40. (B)** Pollen transfer between two different flowers of the same kind is **cross-pollination**.

**Q41. (B)** Speed = distance ÷ time, so its SI unit is **metre per second (m/s)**.

**Q42. (B)** The time period is the time for one complete oscillation, which is **2 s**.

**Q43. (C)** An **electric iron** works on the heating effect of electric current.

**Q44. (C)** By the law of reflection, the angle of incidence is always **equal to the angle of reflection**.

**Q45. (A)** The settled solid waste at the bottom of the settling tank is called **sludge**.

**Q46. (B)** Water is heated by **convection**: warmed water at the bottom expands, becomes less dense, and rises, while cooler denser water sinks. These circulating convection currents distribute heat throughout the beaker, so both thermometers rise.

**Q47. (B)** Rusting requires **both oxygen and moisture**. Nail X is sealed from moisture (grease + drying agent), so it does not rust. Nail Y has water and air, so **Nail Y rusts**.

**Q48. (C)** Total distance = 60 + 60 = 120 km; total time = 1 + 2 = 3 h. Average speed = 120 ÷ 3 = **40 km/h**. (Note: average speed is total distance ÷ total time, not the average of the two speeds.)

**Q49. (B)** The plant loses water vapour by **transpiration**; the vapour then cools and **condenses** into droplets on the inner surface of the bag.

**Q50. (B)** The deflection of the compass needle only when current flows shows that **an electric current produces a magnetic effect** around the wire (Oersted's observation).
