## Detailed Solutions

**Q1.** Each term = previous × 2 + 1: 63 × 2 + 1 = **127**. (Also each term is one less than a power of 2: 2⁷ − 1 = 127.) → **(C)**

**Q2.** A young cat is a kitten; a young cow is a **calf**. → **(B)**

**Q3.** In FROG → GSPH each letter shifts +1 (F→G, R→S, O→P, G→H). So BIRD → C, J, S, E = **CJSE**. → **(A)**

**Q4.** Terms are n(n+1): 1·2=2, 2·3=6, 3·4=12, 4·5=20, 5·6=30, 6·7=**42**. → **(B)**

**Q5.** "My grandfather's only son" is Rohan's father. His daughter is Rohan's **sister**. → **(C)**

**Q6.** A water image is a top-to-bottom (horizontal-axis) reflection. "E" flipped so its bottom edge becomes the top gives the shape in option (A). → **(A)**

**Q7.** Facing East, turn left → North, walk 4 m; turn left again → West. She now faces **West**. → **(B)**

**Q8.** Position from right = Total − Left position + 1 = 30 − 12 + 1 = **19th**. → **(C)**

**Q9.** Pattern: first × second + 1 = third. 7 × 8 + 1 = **57**. → **(C)**

**Q10.** With two cevians from the apex to the base, the base is split into 3 parts giving 3 smallest triangles, plus pairs combine: (1+2), (2+3), and the whole (1+2+3). Total = 3 + 2 + 1 = **6**. → **(D)**

**Q11.** 25 = 5², 49 = 7², 81 = 9² are perfect squares; **63** is not. → **(C)**

**Q12.** For P to be grandfather of Q, we need P × R + Q: P × R means P is father of R, and R + Q means R is mother of Q. So P is the father of R, who is the mother of Q — making P the grandfather of Q. → **(A)**

**Q13.** Folding once gives 2 layers, twice gives 4 layers. One punch through 4 layers = **4 holes** when unfolded. → **(C)**

**Q14.** Gaps increase: B(+2)D(+3)G(+4)K(+5)P(+6)**V**. P + 6 = V. → **(B)**

**Q15.** Facing North, order left→right. Chetan left of Bina, Anil right of Bina, Deepa extreme right, Esha between Anil and Deepa. Order: Chetan, Bina, Anil, Esha, Deepa. Middle (3rd) is **Anil**. → **(A)**

**Q16.** −15 + 23 = 8; 8 + 8 = 16; 16 − 12 = **4**. → **(B)**

**Q17.** −72 ÷ 9 = **−8**. → **(B)**

**Q18.** LCM of 4,6,3 = 12: 9/12 + 10/12 − 8/12 = **11/12**. → **(A)**

**Q19.** As decimals: −3/4 = −0.75, −2/3 ≈ −0.667, −5/6 ≈ −0.833, −7/12 ≈ −0.583. The greatest (closest to zero) is **−7/12**. → **(D)**

**Q20.** 0.75 × 0.4 = **0.30**. → **(A)**

**Q21.** 5x − 3x = 9 + 7 → 2x = 16 → x = **8**. → **(C)**

**Q22.** Supplementary: 180 − 65 = **115°**. → **(C)**

**Q23.** 180 − (47 + 68) = 180 − 115 = **65°**. → **(B)**

**Q24.** Exterior angle = sum of opposite interior angles: 120 − 45 = **75°**. → **(C)**

**Q25.** Two sides and included angle → **SAS**. → **(B)**

**Q26.** 3(2²) − 2(2)(−1) + (−1)² = 12 + 4 + 1 = **17**. → **(D)**

**Q27.** (5x² − 2x²) + (−3x − 4x) + (2 + 5) = 3x² − 7x + 7. → **(A)**

**Q28.** 2^(3+2−4) = 2¹ = **2**. → **(A)**

**Q29.** 45000 = **4.5 × 10⁴**. → **(B)**

**Q30.** 35% of 240 = 0.35 × 240 = **84**. → **(C)**

**Q31.** Perimeter 54 → half = 27 = length + width → width = 27 − 15 = 12. Area = 15 × 12 = **180 cm²**. → **(C)**

**Q32.** Area = ½ × 12 × 9 = **54 cm²**. → **(B)**

**Q33.** Sum = 12+15+18+21+24 = 90; mean = 90/5 = **18**. → **(B)**

**Q34.** An equilateral triangle has **3** lines of symmetry. → **(C)**

**Q35.** A cuboid has **12 edges, 6 faces, 8 vertices**. → **(A)**

**Q36.** 3×45 = 135; 2×18 = 36; total = 171. Change = 200 − 171 = **₹29**. → **(A)**

**Q37.** 15% of 800 = 120; SP = 800 − 120 = **₹680**. → **(B)**

**Q38.** 3/5 × 250 = **150 L**. → **(C)**

**Q39.** Speed = 360/4 = 90 km/h; distance in 6 h = 90 × 6 = **540 km**. → **(C)**

**Q40.** SI = (5000 × 8 × 2)/100 = **₹800**. → **(C)**

**Q41.** Sugar : Flour = 2 : 5. Flour = 400 → one part = 80; sugar = 2 × 80 = **160 g**. → **(C)**

**Q42.** −4 + 11 = 7; 7 − 6 = **1°C**. → **(B)**

**Q43.** Perimeter = 2(40 + 25) = 2 × 65 = **130 m**. → **(C)**

**Q44.** 288 ÷ 24 = **12** days. → **(C)**

**Q45.** Girls = 60% of 40 = 24; boys = 40 − 24 = **16**. → **(B)**

**Q46.** CP = 1200. MP = 1200 × 1.25 = 1500. SP = 1500 × 0.80 = 1200. SP = CP, so **no profit no loss**. → **(C)**

**Q47.** Let son = s, so father = 60 − s. Six years ago: (60 − s − 6) = 3(s − 6) → 54 − s = 3s − 18 → 72 = 4s → s = **18 years**. → **(B)**

**Q48.** In triangle ABC with A = 90°, angle BOC = 90° + (A/2) = 90° + 45° = **135°**. → **(C)**

**Q49.** (2⁴/2²) × (3²/3) × (5/5²) = 2² × 3 × (1/5) = 4 × 3 / 5 = **12/5**. → **(A)**

**Q50.** Outer rectangle = (60 + 2×3) × (40 + 2×3) = 66 × 46 = 3036 m². Field = 60 × 40 = 2400 m². Path = 3036 − 2400 = **636 m²**. → **(B)**
