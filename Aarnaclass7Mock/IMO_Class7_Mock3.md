# SOF IMO — International Mathematics Olympiad
## Class 7 — Mock Test 3

**Total Questions:** 50

**Instructions:**
1. There are 4 sections: Logical Reasoning (Q1–Q15), Mathematical Reasoning (Q16–Q35), Everyday Mathematics (Q36–Q45), and the Achievers Section (Q46–Q50).

## Section 1: Logical Reasoning (Q1–Q15)

**Q1.** Find the next number in the series: 5, 11, 23, 47, 95, ?  
(A) 165  (B) 175  (C) 191  (D) 190

**Q2.** In a certain code language, `LIGHT` is written as `KHFGS`. How is `SOUND` written in that code?  
(A) RNTMC  (B) RNTNC  (C) TPVOE  (D) RMTMC

**Q3.** Choose the correct analogy: `Author : Book :: Sculptor : ?`  
(A) Stone  (B) Chisel  (C) Statue  (D) Museum

**Q4.** Pointing to a man, Sneha said, "His mother is the only daughter of my mother." How is Sneha related to the man?  
(A) Mother  (B) Sister  (C) Aunt  (D) Grandmother

**Q5.** A boy starts walking towards East, turns right, then turns right again, and finally turns left. In which direction is he now facing?  
(A) North  (B) South  (C) East  (D) West

**Q6.** How many times does the digit 7 appear from 1 to 100?  
(A) 10  (B) 11  (C) 19  (D) 20

**Q7.** In a row of 35 children, Karan is 9th from the left and Nisha is 12th from the right. How many children are between them?  
(A) 12  (B) 13  (C) 14  (D) 15

**Q8.** Find the odd one out: 121, 144, 169, 200, 225  
(A) 121  (B) 144  (C) 200  (D) 225

**Q9.** If `P` means `+`, `Q` means `−`, `R` means `×`, and `S` means `÷`, then find the value of: `18 R 2 P 12 S 4 Q 5`  
(A) 34  (B) 32  (C) 36  (D) 30

**Q10.** Complete the series: `AB, DE, GH, JK, ?`  
(A) LM  (B) MN  (C) MO  (D) LN

**Q11.** Six friends A, B, C, D, E, F are seated in a row facing North. C is to the immediate right of D. B is at an extreme end and next to A. F is between A and E. Who is seated at the other extreme end (opposite to B)?  
(A) C  (B) D  (C) E  (D) F

**Q12.** A solid cube is painted blue on all six faces and then cut into 64 identical smaller cubes. How many smaller cubes have exactly two faces painted blue?  
(A) 8  (B) 12  (C) 24  (D) 36

**Q13.** If the 3rd day of a month is a Monday, what day of the week will the 25th of the same month be?  
(A) Monday  (B) Tuesday  (C) Wednesday  (D) Sunday

**Q14.** Choose the number pair that follows the same relationship as `(4, 20)`:  
(A) (6, 30)  (B) (5, 24)  (C) (7, 42)  (D) (3, 12)

**Q15.** A square sheet of paper is folded in half twice (once left-to-right, then top-to-bottom) to form a smaller square. A single triangular notch is cut from the corner of the folded square that corresponds to the original centre of the sheet. When unfolded, how many complete holes appear?  
(A) 1  (B) 2  (C) 3  (D) 4

## Detailed Solutions

**Q1.** Each term = previous × 2 + 1: 95 × 2 + 1 = 191. → **(C)**

**Q2.** Each letter shifts −1: L→K, I→H, G→F, H→G, T→S. So SOUND → R, N, T, M, C = RNTMC. → **(A)**

**Q3.** An author creates a book; a sculptor creates a statue. → **(C)**

**Q4.** "Only daughter of my mother" = Sneha herself; the man's mother is Sneha, so Sneha is the man's mother. → **(A)**

**Q5.** East → right = South → right = West → left = North. He faces North. → **(A)**

**Q6.** Units place 7: 7,17,…,97 = 10 times. Tens place 7: 70–79 = 10 times. Total = 20. → **(D)**

**Q7.** Karan's position from left = 9; Nisha's from left = 35 − 12 + 1 = 24. Between them = 24 − 9 − 1 = 14. → **(C)**

**Q8.** 121, 144, 169, 225 are perfect squares (11², 12², 13², 15²); 200 is not. → **(C)**

**Q9.** 18 × 2 + 12 ÷ 4 − 5 = 36 + 3 − 5 = 34. → **(A)**

**Q10.** Groups skip one letter each time: AB(C)DE(F)GH(I)JK(L)MN. Next = MN. → **(B)**

**Q11.** B is at one end next to A, F between A and E, and D–C together. Order: B, A, F, E, D, C. The opposite extreme end is C. → **(C)**

**Q12.** In a 4×4×4 cube, two-face-painted cubes lie on the edges (not corners): each edge has 4 − 2 = 2 such cubes × 12 edges = 24. → **(C)**

**Q13.** 3rd is Monday, so 10th, 17th, 24th are Mondays; 25th is Tuesday. → **(B)**

**Q14.** 20 = 4 × 5, i.e. second = first × 5. Check: 6 × 5 = 30. → **(A)**

**Q15.** Two folds create 4 layers; a notch at the corner corresponding to the sheet's centre opens to a single hole at the centre. → **(A)**

**Q16.** −18 − (−7) + (−4) = −18 + 7 − 4 = −15. → **(A)**

**Q17.** Let the number be n: (−2/5) − n = −1 → n = (−2/5) + 1 = 3/5. → **(A)**

**Q18.** 4.2 ÷ 0.06 = 420 ÷ 6 = 70. → **(B)**

**Q19.** (2x)/3 = 6 → 2x = 18 → x = 9. → **(B)**

**Q20.** Supplementary → sum 180°. Parts 4+5 = 9; smaller = (4/9) × 180 = 80°. → **(B)**

**Q21.** Base angles = (180 − 40)/2 = 140/2 = 70°. → **(B)**

**Q22.** 3⁴ ÷ 3² = 3² = 9. → **(C)**

**Q23.** 18/24 = (18÷6)/(24÷6) = 3/4. → **(B)**

**Q24.** 35% of n = 84 → n = 84 ÷ 0.35 = 240. → **(C)**

**Q25.** Side = 100/4 = 25 m; area = 25² = 625 m². → **(A)**

**Q26.** 3(2²) − 5(2) + 2 = 12 − 10 + 2 = 4. → **(A)**

**Q27.** Negatives first: −3/4 (−0.75) < −1/3 (−0.33) < 2/5 (0.4) < 1/2 (0.5). → **(A)**

**Q28.** Area = base × height = 15 × 8 = 120 cm². → **(B)**

**Q29.** Ordered: 1, 3, 5, 7, 8, 9, 11. Middle (4th) value = 7. → **(B)**

**Q30.** (−2)³ = −8; (−1)⁵ = −1; (−8) × (−1) = 8. → **(B)**

**Q31.** RHS: the hypotenuse and one side of a right-angled triangle equal those of another. → **(B)**

**Q32.** T, X, O all have symmetry; P has no line of symmetry. → **(C)**

**Q33.** A triangular prism has two triangular faces → 3 + 3 = 6 vertices. → **(B)**

**Q34.** x/15 = 8/20 = 2/5 → x = 15 × 2/5 = 6. → **(B)**

**Q35.** (−48) ÷ (−6) = 8; 5 × 2 = 10; 8 − 10 = −2. → **(A)**

**Q36.** Profit = 50 − 40 = 10; % = 10/40 × 100 = 25%. → **(B)**

**Q37.** 36 ÷ 1.5 = 24 km/h. → **(C)**

**Q38.** SI = (12000 × 9 × 3)/100 = ₹3,240; Amount = 12000 + 3240 = ₹15,240. → **(B)**

**Q39.** 2¼ − ¾ = 9/4 − 3/4 = 6/4 = 1½ litre. → **(A)**

**Q40.** Loser got 45% of 8000 = 0.45 × 8000 = 3,600. → **(B)**

**Q41.** 540 ÷ 12 = 45 minutes. → **(B)**

**Q42.** Sugar : water = 3 : 8; water 24 → multiplier 3 → sugar = 3 × 3 = 9. → **(C)**

**Q43.** Discount = 12% of 15000 = 1800; SP = 15000 − 1800 = ₹13,200. → **(B)**

**Q44.** −6 + 11 = 5; 5 − 7 = −2°C. → **(A)**

**Q45.** 35 × 30 = ₹1,050. → **(B)**

**Q46.** Sum 63 → middle = 21, so integers are 19, 21, 23. Product of smallest × largest = 19 × 23 = 437. → **(B)**

**Q47.** (x+20) + 2x + (3x−20) = 180 → 6x = 180 → x = 30. Angles: 50°, 60°, 70°. Largest = 70°. → **(B)**

**Q48.** SP for 25% profit = 1200 × 1.25 = 1500. SP = 80% of MP → MP = 1500 ÷ 0.80 = ₹1,875. → **(B)**

**Q49.** (i) 9 + (−8) = 1 (Q); (ii) 16 − 16 = 0 (P); (iii) 16 + 9 = 25 (R); (iv) 25 − 27 = −2 (S). → **(A)**

**Q50.** Reciprocal of −5/8 is −8/5 (since (−5/8)(−8/5) = 1), so A is true; R correctly explains why. → **(A)**
