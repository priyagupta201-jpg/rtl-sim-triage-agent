# SOF IMO — International Mathematics Olympiad

**Total Questions:** 50

**Instructions:**
1. There are 4 sections: Logical Reasoning (Q1–Q15), Mathematical Reasoning (Q16–Q35), Everyday Mathematics (Q36–Q45), and Achievers Section (Q46–Q50).

## Section 1: Logical Reasoning (Q1–Q15)

**Q1.** Find the next term in the series: 3, 6, 11, 18, 27, ?  
(A) 36  (B) 38  (C) 40  (D) 42

**Q2.** If in a certain code language, MANGO is written as NBOHP, then how is APPLE written?  
(A) BQQMF  (B) BQPMF  (C) BQQMG  (D) CQQMF

**Q3.** Choose the analogous pair: Foot : Metre :: Second : ?  
(A) Distance  (B) Clock  (C) Time  (D) Hour

**Q4.** Pointing to a photograph, Ravi said, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." How is the girl related to Ravi?  
(A) Sister  (B) Cousin  (C) Aunt  (D) Niece

**Q5.** A man walks 5 km towards South, then turns left and walks 3 km, then turns left again and walks 5 km. In which direction is he now facing?  
(A) South  (B) North  (C) East  (D) West

**Q6.** In a row of 30 children, Aditya is 12th from the left end. What is his position from the right end?  
(A) 18th  (B) 19th  (C) 20th  (D) 17th

**Q7.** Find the missing number:  
7  →  14  →  ?  →  56  (each number is doubled)  
(A) 21  (B) 28  (C) 35  (D) 42

**Q8.** Which letter is the mirror image of the letter **b** when placed against a vertical mirror on the right?  
(A) d  (B) p  (C) q  (D) b

**Q9.** If A = 1, B = 2, C = 3, …, then what is the sum of the letters in the word "CAB"?  
(A) 5  (B) 6  (C) 7  (D) 8

**Q10.** Complete the number series: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, ?  
(A) 30  (B) 36  (C) 42  (D) 49

**Q11.** In a certain code, 5 = 25, 6 = 36, 7 = 49. What does 9 stand for?  
(A) 72  (B) 81  (C) 90  (D) 99

**Q12.** How many triangles are there in a figure formed by a triangle divided by both of its medians from two vertices meeting inside (a triangle with two internal line segments from two vertices to the midpoints of the opposite sides)?  
(A) 5  (B) 6  (C) 8  (D) 9

**Q13.** Rohan is taller than Sita but shorter than Meena. Kabir is taller than Meena. Who is the tallest?  
(A) Rohan  (B) Sita  (C) Meena  (D) Kabir

**Q14.** A piece of paper is folded once in half and a single square hole is punched near the fold. When unfolded, how many holes will there be?  
(A) 1  (B) 2  (C) 3  (D) 4

**Q15.** Find the odd one out: 8, 27, 64, 100, 125  
(A) 27  (B) 64  (C) 100  (D) 125

## Detailed Solutions

**Q1.** Differences are 3, 5, 7, 9, then 11 → 27 + 11 = 38. **(B)**

**Q2.** Each letter shifts +1: M→N, A→B, N→O, G→H, O→P gives NBOHP; APPLE → BQQMF. **(A)**

**Q3.** Foot is a unit of distance; second is a unit of time. **(C)**

**Q4.** Grandfather's only son is Ravi's father; his daughter is Ravi's sister. **(A)**

**Q5.** South, then left → East (3 km), then left → North (5 km). He faces North. **(B)**

**Q6.** Position from right = 30 − 12 + 1 = 19th. **(B)**

**Q7.** Doubling: 14 × 2 = 28. **(B)**

**Q8.** In a vertical mirror, **b** reflects to **d**. **(A)**

**Q9.** C + A + B = 3 + 1 + 2 = 6. **(B)**

**Q10.** These are perfect squares; next is 6² = 36. **(B)**

**Q11.** Each number maps to its square; 9 → 81. **(B)**

**Q12.** Two cevians from two vertices to opposite midpoints create the whole triangle plus smaller regions totaling 8 triangles. **(C)**

**Q13.** Kabir > Meena > Rohan > Sita, so Kabir is tallest. **(D)**

**Q14.** Folding once and punching gives 2 holes when unfolded. **(B)**

**Q15.** 8, 27, 64, 125 are perfect cubes; 100 is not. **(C)**

**Q16.** −15 + 23 − 8 + 5 = 5. **(B)**

**Q17.** (−4)(−3) = 12; 12 × (−2) = −24. **(B)**

**Q18.** Additive inverse of −7/12 is 7/12. **(B)**

**Q19.** 3/4 + 5/6 = 9/12 + 10/12 = 19/12. **(B)**

**Q20.** 0.375 = 375/1000 = 3/8. **(A)**

**Q21.** 3x = 18, so x = 6. **(C)**

**Q22.** x/5 = 5, so x = 25. **(B)**

**Q23.** 90° − 37° = 53°. **(C)**

**Q24.** 180° − (65° + 75°) = 40°. **(B)**

**Q25.** Exterior angle = sum of opposite interiors: 120° − 45° = 75°. **(C)**

**Q26.** (5a − 2a) + (3b + 4b) = 3a + 7b. **(A)**

**Q27.** 2³ × 2² = 2⁵. **(A)**

**Q28.** 81 = 3 × 3 × 3 × 3 = 3⁴. **(B)**

**Q29.** 45 min : 180 min = 1 : 4. **(A)**

**Q30.** 15% of 800 = 0.15 × 800 = ₹120. **(B)**

**Q31.** Side = 48/4 = 12 cm; area = 12² = 144 cm². **(A)**

**Q32.** ½ × 10 × 6 = 30 cm². **(B)**

**Q33.** 4 appears three times (most frequent). **(C)**

**Q34.** An equilateral triangle has 3 lines of symmetry. **(C)**

**Q35.** A cube has 6 faces, 12 edges, 8 vertices. **(A)**

**Q36.** Profit = 2760 − 2400 = 360; % = 360/2400 × 100 = 15%. **(B)**

**Q37.** SI = (5000 × 8 × 2)/100 = ₹800. **(B)**

**Q38.** 3/4 × 6 = 18/4 = 4½ cups. **(B)**

**Q39.** Perimeter = 2(25 + 18) = 86 m; cost = 86 × 30 = ₹2,580. **(A)**

**Q40.** Girls = 60% of 40 = 24; boys = 40 − 24 = 16. **(C)**

**Q41.** −3 + 9 = 6°C. **(A)**

**Q42.** 45/60 × 100 = 75%. **(C)**

**Q43.** Discount = 15% of 1200 = 180; SP = 1200 − 180 = ₹1,020. **(A)**

**Q44.** 240 ÷ 16 = 15 km per litre. **(C)**

**Q45.** 3/5 × 500 = 300 L. **(B)**

**Q46.** Interest to double = 100% in 8 years. To become 4 times, interest = 300%, needing 3 × 8 = 24 years. **(B)**

**Q47.** 8/27 = (2/3)³, so x = 3. **(B)**

**Q48.** Sum of ratio parts = 9; largest = (4/9) × 180° = 80°. **(C)**

**Q49.** Let CP = x. Difference between 105% and 90% is 15% = ₹90, so x = 90/0.15 = ₹600. **(B)**

**Q50.** Outer area = 40 × 30 = 1200 m²; inner = (40−4)(30−4) = 36 × 26 = 936 m²; path = 1200 − 936 = 264 m². **(B)**
