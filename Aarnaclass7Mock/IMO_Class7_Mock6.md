### Instructions

1. There are **4 sections**: Logical Reasoning (Q1–Q15), Mathematical Reasoning (Q16–Q35), Everyday Mathematics (Q36–Q45), and Achievers Section (Q46–Q50).

## Detailed Solutions

**Q1.** Differences are 3, 5, 7, 9, so the next difference is 11. 27 + 11 = **38**. → (B)

**Q2.** Each letter shifts +1: F→G, R→S, O→P, G→H. So LAMP → L+1=M, A+1=B, M+1=N, P+1=Q = **MBNQ**. → (A)

**Q3.** An ear is the organ used to hear; a tongue is the organ used to taste. → (B)

**Q4.** The "only son of my grandmother" is Rohan's father; his daughter is Rohan's **sister**. → (A)

**Q5.** North → (right) East → (right) South. After two right turns from North he faces **South**. → (B)

**Q6.** In a vertical mirror the digits reverse order left-to-right: 1963 → **3691**. → (A)

**Q7.** Total = (position from left) + (position from right) − 1 = 7 + 12 − 1 = **18**. → (B)

**Q8.** Substitute: ÷→+, ×→−, +→×. So 16 + 4 − 2 × 3 = 16 + 4 − 6 = **14**. → (A)

**Q9.** Order tallest to shortest: Sara > Priya > Kiran > Nitin > Deepa. Second tallest is **Priya**. → (B)

**Q10.** A rectangle with both diagonals gives 4 small triangles + 4 larger triangles (each formed by two small ones) = **8**. → (C)

**Q11.** One fold and one punch not on the fold line produces a mirrored hole, giving **2** holes. → (B)

**Q12.** 121=11², 144=12², 169=13², 196=14² are perfect squares; **180** is not. → (C)

**Q13.** Shifting Y two places forward (wrapping around): Y→Z→A, so **A**. → (A)

**Q14.** Rule: (a×b) + (a+b) → 4×5+9 = 29? Check: pattern is a×b + (a+b): 4×5+9=29 (no). Use a²+b²: 16+25=41 ✓; 9+36=45 ✓. So 2,7 → 4+49 = **53**. → (C)

**Q15.** Letters symmetric about a horizontal axis stay the same in a water image. Among the options, **D** has top-bottom symmetry (its curved right side and straight left side are mirror-symmetric across the horizontal centre line). → (C)

**Q16.** (−15) + 24 = 9; 9 + (−9) = 0; 0 + 6 = **6**. → (A)

**Q17.** (−8)(−3) = 24; (−5)(4) = −20; 24 + (−20) = **4**. → (A)

**Q18.** LCM 12: $\frac{9}{12} + \frac{10}{12} - \frac{8}{12} = \frac{11}{12}$. → (A)

**Q19.** 0.25 × 0.4 = 0.100 = **0.1**. → (A)

**Q20.** Midpoint = $\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{2}+\frac{3}{4}\right) = \frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{5}{4} = \frac{5}{8}$. → (A)

**Q21.** 3x − 2x = 5 + 7 → x = **12**. → (B)

**Q22.** $\frac{x}{5} = 5$ → x = **25**. → (B)

**Q23.** Complementary means sum 90°: 90 − 37 = **53°**. → (A)

**Q24.** 180 − (65 + 48) = 180 − 113 = **67°**. → (B)

**Q25.** Exterior angle = sum of two opposite interior angles: 120 − 45 = **75°**. → (B)

**Q26.** Three pairs of equal sides is the **SSS** criterion. → (C)

**Q27.** Discount = 15% of 800 = 120; SP = 800 − 120 = **₹680**. → (A)

**Q28.** 40% of n = 60 → n = 60 × 100 / 40 = **150**. → (B)

**Q29.** (5a − 2a) + (3b + 4b) = **3a + 7b**. → (A)

**Q30.** $2^{3+2-4} = 2^1 = $ **2**. → (A)

**Q31.** 6.4 × 10⁴ = 6.4 × 10000 = **64000**. → (B)

**Q32.** Area = ½ × 14 × 9 = **63 cm²**. → (A)

**Q33.** Side = 48/4 = 12; area = 12² = **144 cm²**. → (A)

**Q34.** 15 appears twice, more than any other value; mode = **15**. → (B)

**Q35.** A regular pentagon has **5** lines of symmetry. → (C)

**Q36.** Cost = 3×45 + 2×18 = 135 + 36 = 171; change = 200 − 171 = **₹29**. → (A)

**Q37.** Used = 5/8 × 240 = 150; remaining = 240 − 150 = **90 litres**. → (A)

**Q38.** 4 h 30 min = 4.5 h; speed = 360 / 4.5 = **80 km/h**. → (B)

**Q39.** SI = 5000 × 8 × 2 / 100 = 800; amount = 5000 + 800 = **₹5800**. → (A)

**Q40.** Perimeter = 2(25 + 18) = 86 m; cost = 86 × 12 = **₹1032**. → (A)

**Q41.** Girls = 60% of 40 = 24; boys = 40 − 24 = **16**. → (A)

**Q42.** Profit = 2880 − 2400 = 480; % = 480/2400 × 100 = **20%**. → (B)

**Q43.** Sugar : flour = 2 : 5, flour = 350 → one part = 70; sugar = 2 × 70 = **140 g**. → (A)

**Q44.** −3 + 11 = 8; 8 − 4 = **4°C**. → (A)

**Q45.** Read = (1/4 + 3/8) × 320 = (2/8 + 3/8) × 320 = 5/8 × 320 = 200; left = 320 − 200 = **120**. → (B)

**Q46.** MP = 500 × 1.4 = 700; SP = 700 × 0.9 = 630; profit = 630 − 500 = 130; % = 130/500 × 100 = **26%**. → (A)

**Q47.** Let integers be n−2, n, n+2: sum = 3n = 96 → n = 32. Integers 30, 32, 34; product of smallest and largest = 30 × 34 = **1020**. → (B)

**Q48.** Co-interior angles are supplementary: (3x+20) + (2x+10) = 180 → 5x + 30 = 180 → x = 30. Angles are 3(30)+20 = 110° and 2(30)+10 = 70°. Larger = **110°**. → (A)

**Q49.** New dimensions: length = 30 − 2×4 = 22, width = 20 − 2×4 = 12, height = 4. Volume = 22 × 12 × 4 = **1408 cm³**. → (D)

**Q50.** $\frac{2^5}{2^3} \times \frac{3^2}{3} \times \frac{7}{7} = 2^2 \times 3 \times 1 = 4 \times 3 = $ **12**. → (C)
