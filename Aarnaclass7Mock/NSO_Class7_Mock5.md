## Detailed Solutions

**Q1. (B)** Each term is (previous × 2 + 1): 63 × 2 + 1 = 127.

**Q2. (A)** Each letter moves forward by 1: F→G, L→M, O→P, W→X, E→F, R→S = GMPXFS.

**Q3. (B)** The ankle joins the foot to the leg; similarly the wrist joins the hand to the arm.

**Q4. (B)** At 3:00 the minute hand is at 12 and the hour hand at 3, three hour-marks apart. Each hour-mark = 30°, so 3 × 30° = 90°.

**Q5. (A)** "Grandfather's only son" is the man's father. His father's daughter is the man's sister.

**Q6. (B)** Differences are 4, 6, 8, 10, 12: 30 + 12 = 42. (Also n(n+1): 6×7 = 42.)

**Q7. (C)** North 5 km, turn right → East 4 km, turn right → South 5 km. The 5 km North and 5 km South cancel out, leaving a net displacement of 4 km due East. So he is to the **East** of his starting point.

**Q8. (A)** Apply the substitutions: 16 ÷ 4 − 2 × 3 + 2 becomes 16 + 4 ÷ 2 − 3 × 2 = 16 + 2 − 6 = 12.

**Q9. (D)** 121 = 11², 144 = 12², 169 = 13² are perfect squares; 150 is not.

**Q10. (C)** The two small triangles ABM and ACM, plus the whole triangle ABC, give a total of 3 triangles.

**Q11. (A)** In a vertical mirror to the right, letter order reverses and each letter is flipped left-to-right, so CODING reads as G-N-I-D-O-C with each glyph mirrored.

**Q12. (B)** Position from right = Total − Position from left + 1 = 40 − 12 + 1 = 29.

**Q13. (B)** A↔Z, B↔Y (each pair sums to 27 in position). For C(3) the partner is X(24); next CX pattern gives D(4) with W(23) → DW.

**Q14. (A)** 61 ÷ 7 = 8 remainder 5, so the day is 5 days ahead of Wednesday. Wed +1 = Thu, +2 = Fri, +3 = Sat, +4 = Sun, +5 = **Monday**.

**Q15. (A)** The dot moves clockwise: top-left → top-right → bottom-right → bottom-left.

**Q16. (B)** Chlorophyll, the green pigment in leaves, captures light energy for photosynthesis.

**Q17. (C)** Cuscuta lacks chlorophyll and draws nutrients from a host plant, so it is a parasite.

**Q18. (B)** A lichen is a symbiotic partnership between a fungus and an alga.

**Q19. (B)** Digestion breaks complex food into simple soluble substances.

**Q20. (A)** In ruminants, swallowed grass is stored and cellulose is broken down in the rumen.

**Q21. (C)** The SI unit of temperature is the kelvin (K).

**Q22. (A)** In conduction, heat passes particle to particle without the particles themselves travelling.

**Q23. (B)** A clinical thermometer reads about 35 °C to 42 °C to cover human body temperature.

**Q24. (B)** Dark surfaces absorb more heat/radiation, keeping the body warmer in winter.

**Q25. (A)** Turmeric is yellow in acids/neutral and turns **reddish-brown/red** in bases. On a base, turmeric paper turns red.

**Q26. (C)** Car batteries use sulphuric acid, a strong mineral acid.

**Q27. (B)** Acid + base → salt + water is neutralisation.

**Q28. (B)** Rusting forms a new substance (iron oxide), a chemical change; the others are physical.

**Q29. (B)** CaO + water → calcium hydroxide, a chemical change that releases heat (exothermic).

**Q30. (C)** Galvanisation coats iron with zinc to prevent rusting.

**Q31. (A)** Polar animals have a thick fat layer called blubber for insulation.

**Q32. (B)** Rainforest animals have strong tails and gripping limbs to climb and move among trees.

**Q33. (B)** Wind flows from high-pressure to low-pressure regions.

**Q34. (A)** Rising warm moist air cools, water vapour condenses, and clouds form.

**Q35. (B)** Warm-sea low-pressure systems with rotating winds are cyclones.

**Q36. (B)** Decomposed organic matter in soil is humus.

**Q37. (B)** Clayey soil holds water well, making it ideal for paddy.

**Q38. (B)** Gaseous exchange in lungs occurs in the alveoli.

**Q39. (C)** Earthworms respire through their moist skin.

**Q40. (A)** Plasma is the pale-yellow liquid part of blood.

**Q41. (B)** Xylem carries water and minerals upward from roots.

**Q42. (C)** Transfer of pollen from anther to stigma is pollination.

**Q43. (A)** Equal distance in equal time is uniform motion.

**Q44. (C)** An electric iron works on the heating effect of current.

**Q45. (B)** A convex mirror gives an erect, diminished image and a wider field of view, ideal as a rear-view mirror.

**Q46. (B)** Time for first 60 km = 60/30 = 2 h; second 60 km = 60/60 = 1 h. Total distance 120 km in 3 h → average speed = 120/3 = 40 km/h.

**Q47. (B)** Red litmus turns blue in bases. Soap solution (ii) and baking soda solution (iv) are basic; lemon juice is acidic and distilled water is neutral.

**Q48. (B)** The plant loses water vapour by transpiration; the vapour then condenses into droplets on the cooler inner walls of the box.

**Q49. (B)** Adding a bulb in series increases total resistance, lowering the current, so each bulb (including the original) glows dimmer.

**Q50. (B)** Metals expand on heating. The heated ball expanded and no longer fits. Heating the ring makes it expand as well, enlarging its hole so the ball can pass through again.
