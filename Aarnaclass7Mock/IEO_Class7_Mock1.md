### Passage 1 (Q21–Q25)

Read the passage carefully and answer the questions that follow.

> The honeybee is one of nature's most hard-working insects. A single hive can contain up to sixty thousand bees, each with a special task. The queen bee is the largest and her only job is to lay eggs — sometimes as many as two thousand a day. The worker bees, all female, gather nectar, build the honeycomb, clean the hive and guard the entrance. The male bees, called drones, do very little work; their main role is to help the queen produce young.
>
> When a worker bee finds a good source of flowers, she returns to the hive and performs a special "waggle dance." By moving in a figure-of-eight pattern and shaking her body, she tells the other bees the direction and distance of the flowers. In this way, thousands of bees can be guided to food without ever speaking a word. Scientists believe this dance is one of the most remarkable forms of communication in the animal world.
