## Detailed Solutions

**Q1.** Differences are 3, 5, 7, 9, next is 11 → 27 + 11 = **38**.

**Q2.** Each letter moves +1: M→N, O→P, N→O, D→E, A→B, Y→Z. So FRIDAY → F+1=G, R+1=S, I+1=J, D+1=E, A+1=B, Y+1=Z = **GSJEBZ**.

**Q3.** A peacock is a type of bird; a cobra is a type of **reptile**.

**Q4.** "Only son of my grandfather" is Rohan's father; his daughter is Rohan's **sister**.

**Q5.** South, left → East, left → North. Facing **North**.

**Q6.** 6² + 7² = 36 + 49 = **85**.

**Q7.** 121, 144, 169 are perfect squares (11², 12², 13²); 150 is not → **150**.

**Q8.** Position from right = (30 − 12) + 1 = **19th**.

**Q9.** A rectangle with both diagonals gives 8 triangles (4 small + 4 larger halves). = **8**.

**Q10.** Substitute: `12 × 3 ÷ 6 − 2 + 4` = 36 ÷ 6 = 6; 6 − 2 = wait, `−` means ÷: `6 ÷ 2` = 3? Re-map carefully: given +→×, ×→−, −→÷, ÷→+. Expression `12 + 3 − 6 × 2 ÷ 4` becomes `12 × 3 ÷ 6 − 2 + 4`. Apply BODMAS: 12×3 = 36, 36÷6 = 6, 6−2… but `−` here is the real subtraction symbol after conversion, and ÷ became +. So `6 − 2 + 4` = 6 − 2 = 4, +4 = **10**.

**Q11.** In a vertical mirror the word reverses left-to-right to read `MAXE` with each letter laterally flipped → **(A)**.

**Q12.** Letters decrease by 3: Z, W, T, Q, N, **K**.

**Q13.** C is mother of A and B; D is father of C, so D is grandfather of A and B. B is D's **grandchild (granddaughter or grandson)**.

**Q14.** Day before yesterday = Thursday, so today = Saturday. Tomorrow = Sunday, and the day after tomorrow = **Monday** → (B).

**Q15.** Each number is squared: 11² = **121**.

**Q16.** Green leaves make food by **photosynthesis** using sunlight, CO₂ and water.

**Q17.** *Cuscuta* has no chlorophyll and takes food from a host — a **parasitic** plant.

**Q18.** *Rhizobium* bacteria in root nodules fix atmospheric nitrogen.

**Q19.** Feeding on dead/decaying matter is **saprotrophic** nutrition.

**Q20.** Taking in and using food is **nutrition**.

**Q21.** Ruminants store swallowed grass in the **rumen** and later chew it as cud.

**Q22.** SI unit of temperature is the **kelvin**.

**Q23.** A clinical thermometer reads about **35 °C to 42 °C**.

**Q24.** A metal spoon conducts heat — transfer is by **conduction**.

**Q25.** **White** reflects most heat and absorbs the least, keeping us cool.

**Q26.** Turmeric turns red in a **basic** substance.

**Q27.** Acid + base → salt + water is **neutralisation**.

**Q28.** Acidic soil is treated with **quick lime / slaked lime** (a base).

**Q29.** **Rusting of iron** forms a new substance — a chemical change.

**Q30.** Iron + dilute HCl → iron chloride + **hydrogen** gas.

**Q31.** Coating iron with zinc is **galvanisation**.

**Q32.** Polar bears have **blubber and thick fur** for cold climates.

**Q33.** Migratory birds move to places with **favourable weather and food**.

**Q34.** Heated air **expands and becomes less dense**, so it rises.

**Q35.** A cyclone is **high-speed winds revolving around a low-pressure centre**.

**Q36.** Decayed matter forms **humus** in soil.

**Q37.** **Sandy soil** has large particles and the highest percolation rate.

**Q38.** Gas exchange happens in the **alveoli** of the lungs.

**Q39.** The **earthworm** respires through its moist skin.

**Q40.** The pale-yellow fluid part of blood is **plasma**.

**Q41.** Water and minerals rise through the **xylem**.

**Q42.** Pollen transfer from anther to stigma is **pollination**.

**Q43.** Winged seeds are dispersed by **wind**.

**Q44.** **Unequal distances in equal time intervals** means non-uniform motion.

**Q45.** The heating effect of current is used in an **electric heater / fuse**.

**Q46.** Both statements are true and the differential heating of land vs sea is exactly why the sea breeze forms → **(A)**.

**Q47.** **Copper** is the best conductor among the four, so its wax melts first and the pin falls first.

**Q48.** Time period = time for one complete oscillation = to-and-fro. One extreme to the other is half an oscillation (2 s), so the full period = **4 seconds**.

**Q49.** P turns blue litmus red → acidic. Q turns red litmus blue → basic. Adding a base to an acid causes neutralisation, giving a **neutral** salt-and-water mixture → **(B)**.

**Q50.** Stomata are the pores for gas exchange and water vapour loss, so blocking them most directly reduces **photosynthesis (CO₂ intake) and transpiration** → **(A)**.
