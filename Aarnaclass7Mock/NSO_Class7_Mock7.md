## Detailed Solutions

**Q1. (B)** The differences are +3, +5, +7, +9 (odd numbers). Next difference is +11, so 27 + 11 = 38.

**Q2. (A)** Each letter moves forward by one: C→D, A→B, T→U gives DBU. Similarly D→E, O→P, G→H gives **EPH**.

**Q3. (A)** The code is the reverse alphabet (A↔Z, B↔Y …). G→T, A→Z, R→I, D→W, E→V, N→M gives **TZIWVM**.

**Q4. (C)** 12 × ? = 96 → ? = 96 ÷ 12 = **8**.

**Q5. (A)** "My grandfather's only son" is Ravi's father. His daughter is Ravi's **sister**.

**Q6. (A)** Start facing South; walk South. Turn left → face East, walk 3 km. Turn left again → face **North**.

**Q7. (B)** One median divides the big triangle into 2 small triangles, plus the original large triangle = **3** triangles.

**Q8. (D)** 121, 144, 169 are perfect squares (11², 12², 13²). 150 is not a perfect square, so it is the odd one.

**Q9. (B)** Substitute: 18 × 2 + 9 ÷ 3 − 4 = 36 + 3 − 4 = **35**.

**Q10. (A)** The letters skip one each time going backward: Z, X, V, T, R, then **P**.

**Q11. (C)** Position from right = total − position from left + 1 = 25 − 11 + 1 = **15th**.

**Q12. (A)** A vertical mirror reverses left-right and flips the letters. BOOK becomes the reversed, laterally-inverted form **ꓘOOꓭ**.

**Q13. (C)** These are triangular numbers with differences +2, +3, +4, +5, +6. So 15 + 6 = **21**.

**Q14. (B)** B = 2, A = 1, D = 4; sum = 2 + 1 + 4 = **7**.

**Q15. (D)** Order: Sunil > Aman > Rohan > Vikas. The tallest is **Sunil**.

**Q16. (B)** Cuscuta (Amarbel) is a parasitic plant with no chlorophyll; it takes food from a host plant.

**Q17. (A)** Chlorophyll is the green pigment that traps sunlight for photosynthesis.

**Q18. (C)** Amoeba engulfs food using temporary finger-like extensions called pseudopodia.

**Q19. (C)** The SI unit of temperature is the **Kelvin (K)**.

**Q20. (B)** A clinical thermometer typically reads from **35 °C to 42 °C**, covering the human body range.

**Q21. (C)** In solids heat travels by conduction, from particle to particle without the particles moving.

**Q22. (B)** Turmeric is yellow in acids/neutral solutions and turns **red** in a base.

**Q23. (B)** Ant stings and nettle leaves contain **formic acid** (methanoic acid).

**Q24. (B)** Acid + base → salt + water is called **neutralisation**.

**Q25. (B)** Rusting of iron forms a new substance (iron oxide) and is a chemical change. The others are physical changes.

**Q26. (C)** Rusting needs **both oxygen and water (moisture)** to occur.

**Q27. (B)** The thick insulating fat layer in polar animals is called **blubber**.

**Q28. (B)** An **anemometer** measures wind speed.

**Q29. (B)** A cyclone forms over warm oceans where warm moist air rises, creating a **low-pressure** centre that draws in surrounding air.

**Q30. (B)** Decomposer **microorganisms** rot dead matter to form humus, enriching the soil.

**Q31. (C)** **Clayey soil** holds water well and is ideal for paddy cultivation.

**Q32. (B)** Aerobic respiration breaks glucose using oxygen to release **energy, carbon dioxide and water**.

**Q33. (B)** During heavy exercise, muscles respire anaerobically, forming **lactic acid**, which causes cramps.

**Q34. (A)** The liquid part of blood is **plasma**, which is pale yellow.

**Q35. (C)** **Platelets** help blood to clot at a wound.

**Q36. (B)** **Xylem** carries water and minerals upward from roots to leaves.

**Q37. (B)** Transfer of pollen from anther to stigma is **pollination**.

**Q38. (B)** Growing a new potato plant from the buds (eyes) of a tuber is vegetative (asexual) propagation.

**Q39. (B)** Distance per unit time is **speed**.

**Q40. (B)** The time period is the time for one complete oscillation = **2 s**.

**Q41. (B)** The **electric iron** works on the heating effect of current.

**Q42. (B)** An **electromagnet** works on the magnetic effect of electric current.

**Q43. (B)** A plane mirror forms a **virtual, erect image of the same size**, laterally inverted.

**Q44. (B)** A concave mirror gives a magnified erect image when close, so it is used as a **shaving/make-up mirror** and to focus light in torches.

**Q45. (A)** The settled solid at the bottom of the sedimentation tank is **sludge**.

**Q46. (B)** Time for first 60 km = 60/30 = 2 h; time for next 60 km = 60/60 = 1 h. Total distance = 120 km, total time = 3 h. Average speed = 120/3 = **40 km/h**. (Note: it is not the simple average of the two speeds.)

**Q47. (B)** P turns blue litmus red → acidic. Q turns red litmus blue → basic. R has no effect → neutral. S turns blue litmus red and releases hydrogen with a metal → acidic. Hence **P and S are acidic, Q is basic, R is neutral**.

**Q48. (A)** Current can still flow through the circuit and warm the wire, but if the bulb's filament is broken the bulb will not light. So the fused filament is the likely cause.

**Q49. (B)** Water has a much higher heat capacity, so for the same heat input its temperature rises less; **water needs more heat to warm by the same amount**, so the oil gets hotter.

**Q50. (B)** Starving the plant removes stored starch. The part covered by black paper gets no light, so it makes no starch and stays brown with iodine, while the exposed part turns blue-black. This proves **sunlight is necessary for photosynthesis (starch formation)**.
