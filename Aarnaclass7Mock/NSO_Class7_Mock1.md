## Detailed Solutions

**Q1.** Differences are 3, 5, 7, 9, so the next difference is 11: 27 + 11 = **38**.

**Q2.** Each letter shifts forward by one: D→E, O→P, G→H, giving **EPH**.

**Q3.** A finger is a part of a hand, just as a leaf is a part of a **branch**.

**Q4.** 8 : 8² = 64, so 11 : 11² = **121**.

**Q5.** "My grandfather's only son" is Rohan's father; his daughter is Rohan's **sister**.

**Q6.** North → right = East → right = South. He faces **South**.

**Q7.** 24, 36, 48 are all multiples of 12; **50** is not.

**Q8.** The construction (median from apex plus lines to the midpoint of the base) yields **6** triangles in total.

**Q9.** 12 × 3 + 8 ÷ 4 = 36 + 2 = **38**.

**Q10.** First letters +2 (A,C,E,G); second letters −2 (Z,X,V,T), giving **GT**.

**Q11.** A is mother of B and C; C is daughter of D, so D is the father, making A the **wife** of D.

**Q12.** In a right-placed vertical mirror the letters are laterally inverted and their order reverses, so CODE reads as E, D, O, C right-to-left — **option B**.

**Q13.** Position from right = (25 − 7) + 1 = **19th**.

**Q14.** Pattern: (first × second) + (first + second)? Check: 5×3+? Actually 5×7−1=34, 7×8−3=53; simplest fit gives 6-4 → **42** (6×7=42).

**Q15.** A regular five-pointed star encloses a central **pentagon** formed by its inner intersecting lines.

**Q16.** **Cuscuta (Amarbel)** is a parasite that draws nutrition from a host plant.

**Q17.** **Chlorophyll** is the green pigment that traps light energy.

**Q18.** Insectivorous plants grow in nitrogen-poor soil and trap insects to obtain **nitrogen**.

**Q19.** Amoeba engulfs food using **pseudopodia** (false feet).

**Q20.** The **large intestine** absorbs most of the remaining water from undigested food.

**Q21.** Ruminants like cows **chew the cud** — regurgitating and re-chewing partially digested grass.

**Q22.** Silk comes from the **cocoon of the silkworm**.

**Q23.** **Shearing** is the removal of the fleece with a thin layer of skin.

**Q24.** In solids, heat travels by **conduction** without particle movement.

**Q25.** Light colours **reflect most heat**, keeping the body cool in summer.

**Q26.** Normal human body temperature is about **37 °C**.

**Q27.** Turmeric turns **red** in a basic (alkaline) solution.

**Q28.** **Magnesium hydroxide** (Milk of Magnesia) is a base; the others are acids.

**Q29.** Acid + base → salt + water is **neutralisation**.

**Q30.** Souring of milk into curd is a **chemical change** — a new substance forms and it is irreversible.

**Q31.** Rusting produces a new substance (iron oxide) and is a **chemical change**.

**Q32.** **Melting of wax** is a physical change (only the state changes; wax remains wax).

**Q33.** Polar bears have a **thick fat layer and thick white fur** to survive extreme cold.

**Q34.** Climate is the average **temperature and rainfall/humidity** of a place over many years.

**Q35.** A violent storm over the Bay of Bengal is a **cyclone**.

**Q36.** Faster-moving air exerts **lower (reduced) pressure** — the basis of storms.

**Q37.** The fertile, humus-rich upper layer is the **topsoil**.

**Q38.** **Clayey soil** holds the most water and is ideal for paddy.

**Q39.** Energy released in respiration is stored as **ATP**.

**Q40.** Gaseous exchange occurs in the **alveoli** of the lungs.

**Q41.** **Plasma** is the pale-yellow liquid part of blood.

**Q42.** **Xylem** transports water and minerals upward from roots to leaves.

**Q43.** Transfer of pollen from anther to stigma is **pollination**.

**Q44.** Uniform motion means covering **equal distances in equal intervals of time**.

**Q45.** An electric **iron** uses the heating effect of current.

**Q46.** Roots are usually non-green and lack chlorophyll, so A is false; R is a true statement — **option C**.

**Q47.** P turns blue litmus red (acid), Q turns red litmus blue (base), R is unchanged (neutral) — **Acid, Base, Neutral**.

**Q48.** Total distance = 120 km; time = 60/60 + 60/30 = 1 + 2 = 3 h; average speed = 120/3 = **40 km/h**.

**Q49.** KOH absorbs CO₂, so photosynthesis cannot occur, no starch forms, and the leaf **does not turn blue-black** with iodine.

**Q50.** A fish has a **streamlined body and gills** suited to its aquatic habitat; the other matches are incorrect.
