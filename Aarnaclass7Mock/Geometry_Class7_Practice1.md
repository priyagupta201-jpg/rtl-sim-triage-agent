# Class 7 — Mathematics Practice Paper

### Geometry: Transversal Angles • Congruence of Triangles • Exterior Angle Property

**Total Questions:** 40  **Total Marks:** 50  **Time:** 90 minutes

**Instructions:**
1. Section A (Q1–Q20) carries 1 mark each; Section B (Q21–Q32) carries 2 marks each; Section C (Q33–Q40) carries marks as shown.
2. All figures are described in words — read each description carefully and sketch it in your rough work before solving.
3. Show your working for Sections B and C. In congruence questions, always state the correct correspondence of vertices (e.g. triangle ABC ≅ triangle PQR).
4. Take all straight lines as straight (angles on a straight line add to 180°) and treat "parallel" lines as truly parallel.

> **Formulae / Key facts to remember**
> - **Parallel lines cut by a transversal:** corresponding angles equal; alternate interior angles equal; alternate exterior angles equal; co-interior (allied/consecutive) angles are supplementary (add to 180°).
> - **Congruence criteria:** SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, and RHS (right angle–hypotenuse–side). Equal corresponding parts of congruent triangles: **CPCT**.
> - **Angle sum of a triangle = 180°.**
> - **Exterior angle of a triangle = sum of the two interior opposite angles.**
> - Angles on a straight line add to 180°; vertically opposite angles are equal.

## Section A — Multiple Choice (Q1–Q20, 1 mark each)

**Q1.** When two lines are cut by a transversal, a pair of corresponding angles lie:
(A) on the same side of the transversal, one interior and one exterior

**Q3.** Co-interior (allied) angles formed when a transversal cuts two parallel lines are:

(A) 25°  (B) 65°  (C) 115°  (D) 130°

(A) 70°  (B) 110°  (C) 90°  (D) 55°

**Q6.** If a pair of alternate interior angles are equal for two lines cut by a transversal, then the two lines are:
(A) perpendicular  (B) intersecting  (C) parallel  (D) coincident

**Q7.** Vertically opposite angles are always:
(A) supplementary  (B) complementary  (C) equal  (D) 90°

**Q8.** The sum of the three interior angles of any triangle is:
(A) 90°  (B) 180°  (C) 270°  (D) 360°

**Q9.** An exterior angle of a triangle is equal to:
(A) the sum of all three interior angles
(B) the sum of the two interior opposite angles

**Q10.** In triangle ABC, angle A = 50° and angle B = 60°. The exterior angle at C equals:
(A) 70°  (B) 110°  (C) 120°  (D) 130°

**Q11.** An exterior angle of a triangle is 120° and one interior opposite angle is 45°. The other interior opposite angle is:
(A) 60°  (B) 75°  (C) 85°  (D) 165°

**Q12.** Which condition is NOT a valid criterion for the congruence of two triangles?
(A) SSS  (B) SAS  (C) ASA  (D) SSA

**Q13.** "AAA" (three pairs of equal angles) guarantees that two triangles are:
(A) congruent  (B) similar in shape but not necessarily congruent

**Q14.** The RHS congruence criterion applies only to triangles that are:
(A) equilateral  (B) isosceles  (C) right-angled  (D) obtuse-angled

**Q15.** CPCT stands for:
(A) Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles

(C) Congruent Parts of Common Triangles

**Q16.** In triangles ABC and PQR, AB = PQ, BC = QR and AC = PR. The criterion that proves them congruent is:
(A) SAS  (B) ASA  (C) SSS  (D) RHS

**Q17.** In triangles ABC and DEF, angle B = angle E = 90°, hypotenuse AC = hypotenuse DF and BC = EF. The correct criterion is:
(A) SSS  (B) SAS  (C) ASA  (D) RHS

**Q18.** In triangle ABC, angle A = 40° and angle B = 40°. Triangle ABC is:
(A) scalene  (B) right-angled  (C) isosceles  (D) equilateral

**Q19.** If triangle ABC ≅ triangle DEF, then side AB corresponds to:

(A) 40°  (B) 50°  (C) 60°  (D) 130°

## Section B — Find the Value / Short Answer (Q21–Q32, 2 marks each)

**Q21.** Lines l and m are parallel and are cut by a transversal t. At the upper intersection, one angle measures 70°. The angle at the lower intersection that **corresponds** to it is x°. Find x.

**Q22.** Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. One interior angle at the top is 75°. Its **alternate interior angle** at the bottom is x°. Find x.

**Q23.** Lines p and q are parallel, cut by transversal r. A pair of **co-interior (allied) angles** are (x)° and 115°. Find x.

**Q24.** Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. One angle is 3x° and the corresponding angle is 120°. Find x.

**Q25.** Lines a and b are parallel. A transversal makes a co-interior pair measuring (2x + 10)° and (x + 20)°. Using the co-interior property, find x.

**Q26.** In triangle ABC, angle A = 55° and angle B = 65°. The side BC is extended beyond C to point D, forming exterior angle ACD = x°. Find x.

**Q27.** In triangle PQR, the exterior angle at R (formed by extending QR beyond R) is 130°. Angle P = 70°. Find angle Q.

**Q28.** In triangle ABC, the exterior angle at B is (2x)° and the two interior opposite angles are 50° and 70°. Find x.

**Q29.** In triangle ABC, angle A = angle B and the exterior angle at C is 100°. Find angle A.

**Q30.** In triangles ABC and DEF: AB = DE, angle A = angle D, and AC = DF. State the congruence criterion that proves triangle ABC ≅ triangle DEF.

**Q31.** In triangles LMN and XYZ: angle M = angle Y, MN = YZ, and angle N = angle Z. State the congruence criterion that proves triangle LMN ≅ triangle XYZ.

**Q32.** In right triangles ABC and PQR: angle B = angle Q = 90°, hypotenuse AC = hypotenuse PR, and AB = PQ. State the congruence criterion that proves them congruent.

## Section C — Reasoning / Application (Q33–Q40)

**Q33.** (3 marks) Lines l and m are parallel, cut by a transversal. At the upper intersection the transversal makes an angle of 125° (measured on the upper-left). Find (a) the corresponding angle at the lower intersection, (b) the co-interior angle at the lower intersection on the same (left) side, and (c) the alternate interior angle. Give reasons.

**Q34.** (3 marks) In triangle ABC, side BC is extended to D. The exterior angle ACD = 3x°, and the interior opposite angles are angle A = (x + 20)° and angle B = (x + 30)°. Find x and hence each of the three angles A, B, C of the triangle. Verify the angle sum.

**Q35.** (3 marks) Two parallel lines l and m are cut by a transversal at points P (on l) and Q (on m). At P, the angle between the transversal and line l on the lower-right is 65°. A triangle is formed with base on line m: at Q the interior angle of the triangle equals the alternate interior angle from P, and the third angle of the triangle is 80°. Find all three angles of the triangle.

**Q36.** (3 marks) In triangle ABC, angle A = 2x, angle B = 3x, and the exterior angle at C (extending BC beyond C) is 100°. Find x, then find angle A and angle B, and finally angle C.

**Q37.** (3 marks) In triangles ABC and DEF you are told: AB = DE = 6 cm, angle B = angle E = 50°, and BC = EF = 8 cm. (a) State which criterion proves congruence and write the correct congruence statement. (b) Using CPCT, what can you conclude about side AC and angle A?

**Q38.** (3 marks) In a figure, AB and CD are two straight lines intersecting at O. It is given OA = OB, OC = OD (O is the midpoint of both segments). Prove that triangle AOC ≅ triangle BOD, stating the criterion and the equal parts used (hint: use vertically opposite angles).

**Q39.** (3 marks) Lines l and m are parallel. A transversal cuts l at A and m at B. On the same side of the transversal, the two co-interior angles are (5x − 5)° at A and (3x + 25)° at B. Find x, then find both co-interior angles and confirm they are supplementary.

**Q40.** (3 marks) In triangle ABC, AB = AC (isosceles). The bisector AD is drawn from A to BC so that angle BAD = angle CAD, and AD is common. Prove that triangle ABD ≅ triangle ACD, stating the criterion and the three equal parts. Using CPCT, state what you can conclude about BD and DC.

## Detailed Solutions

**Q1.** Corresponding angles sit at matching positions: same side of the transversal, one interior and one exterior. **(A)**

**Q2.** Alternate interior angles of parallel lines are equal. **(B)**

**Q3.** Co-interior (allied) angles of parallel lines are supplementary (sum 180°). **(C)**

**Q4.** Corresponding angles are equal, so the angle = 65°. **(B)**

**Q5.** Co-interior angles are supplementary: 180° − 110° = 70°. **(A)**

**Q6.** Equal alternate interior angles is the test for the lines being parallel. **(C)**

**Q7.** Vertically opposite angles are always equal. **(C)**

**Q8.** Angle sum of a triangle is 180°. **(B)**

**Q9.** Exterior angle = sum of the two interior opposite angles. **(B)**

**Q10.** Exterior angle at C = angle A + angle B = 50° + 60° = 110°. **(B)**

**Q11.** 120° = 45° + other, so other = 120° − 45° = 75°. **(B)**

**Q12.** SSA is not a valid congruence criterion. **(D)**

**Q13.** AAA gives similar (same shape) triangles, not necessarily congruent. **(B)**

**Q14.** RHS applies only to right-angled triangles. **(C)**

**Q15.** CPCT = Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles. **(A)**

**Q16.** All three sides equal → SSS. **(C)**

**Q17.** Right angle, hypotenuse and one side equal → RHS. **(D)**

**Q18.** Two equal angles (40°, 40°) → the sides opposite them are equal → isosceles. **(C)**

**Q19.** In ABC ≅ DEF, A↔D and B↔E, so AB ↔ DE. **(C)**

**Q20.** Third angle = 180° − (35° + 95°) = 180° − 130° = 50°. **(B)**

**Q21.** Corresponding angles are equal → x = **70**.

**Q22.** Alternate interior angles are equal → x = **75**.

**Q23.** Co-interior angles are supplementary: x + 115 = 180 → x = **65**.

**Q24.** Corresponding angles are equal: 3x = 120 → x = **40**.

**Q25.** Co-interior angles add to 180°: (2x + 10) + (x + 20) = 180 → 3x + 30 = 180 → 3x = 150 → x = **50**. (Angles are 110° and 70°, which sum to 180°.)

**Q26.** Exterior angle at C = angle A + angle B = 55° + 65° = **120°**.

**Q27.** Exterior angle at R = angle P + angle Q → 130° = 70° + angle Q → angle Q = **60°**.

**Q28.** 2x = 50 + 70 = 120 → x = **60**.

**Q29.** angle A = angle B; exterior angle at C = angle A + angle B = 2·angle A = 100° → angle A = **50°**.

**Q30.** Two sides (AB = DE, AC = DF) and the **included** angle (angle A = angle D) are equal → **SAS**. So triangle ABC ≅ triangle DEF.

**Q31.** Two angles (angle M = angle Y, angle N = angle Z) and the **included** side (MN = YZ) are equal → **ASA**. So triangle LMN ≅ triangle XYZ.

**Q32.** Right angle, equal hypotenuse and one equal side → **RHS**. So triangle ABC ≅ triangle PQR.

**Q33.** (a) Corresponding angle = 125° (corresponding angles equal). (b) Co-interior angle on the same side = 180° − 125° = 55° (co-interior angles supplementary). (c) Alternate interior angle = 55° (equal to the co-interior partner's supplement / alternate interior angles equal). **Answers: 125°, 55°, 55°.**

**Q34.** Exterior angle = sum of interior opposite angles: 3x = (x + 20) + (x + 30) → 3x = 2x + 50 → x = **25**. Then angle A = 45°, angle B = 55°, exterior angle C = 3x = 75°, so interior angle C = 180° − 75° = 80° (equivalently 180° − 45° − 55° = 80°). Check: 45° + 55° + 80° = 180°. ✓

**Q35.** At P the angle with line l (lower-right) is 65°; its alternate interior angle at Q inside the triangle is 65° (alternate interior angles equal, since l ∥ m). Given third angle = 80°. Remaining angle = 180° − 65° − 80° = 35°. **Three angles: 65°, 35°, 80°** (sum 180°). ✓

**Q36.** Exterior angle at C = angle A + angle B → 100° = 2x + 3x = 5x → x = **20**. So angle A = 2x = 40°, angle B = 3x = 60°, and angle C = 180° − 40° − 60° = **80°** (also 180° − 100° = 80°). ✓

**Q37.** (a) AB = DE, included angle B = angle E, BC = EF → **SAS**; congruence statement: **triangle ABC ≅ triangle DEF**. (b) By CPCT, **AC = DF** and **angle A = angle D**.

**Q38.** In triangles AOC and BOD: OA = OB (given), OC = OD (given), and angle AOC = angle BOD (vertically opposite angles). Two sides and the included angle are equal → **SAS** → triangle AOC ≅ triangle BOD.

**Q39.** Co-interior angles are supplementary: (5x − 5) + (3x + 25) = 180 → 8x + 20 = 180 → 8x = 160 → x = **20**. Angles: 5(20) − 5 = 95° and 3(20) + 25 = 85°. Check: 95° + 85° = 180°. ✓ (Supplementary, as required.)

**Q40.** In triangles ABD and ACD: AB = AC (given), angle BAD = angle CAD (AD bisects angle A), AD = AD (common). Two sides and the included angle are equal → **SAS** → triangle ABD ≅ triangle ACD. By CPCT, **BD = DC** (so D is the midpoint of BC).
