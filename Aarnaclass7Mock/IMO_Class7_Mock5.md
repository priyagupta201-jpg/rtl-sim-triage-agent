## Detailed Solutions

**Q1.** Each term doubles: 48 × 2 = **96**. (C)

**Q2.** Each letter shifts +1: T→U, A→B, B→C, L→M, E→F = **UBCMF**. (A)

**Q3.** A foot belongs to a man; a hoof belongs to a **horse**. (B)

**Q4.** Each term doubles: 56 × 2 = **112**. (C)

**Q5.** "Only son of my grandmother" is Rohan's father; his daughter is Rohan's **sister**. (B)

**Q6.** North → right = East (walk 3) → right = South. He now faces **South**. (B)

**Q7.** Position from right = 25 − 11 + 1 = **15th**. (B)

**Q8.** 6² + 7² = 36 + 49 = **85**. (B)

**Q9.** A vertical mirror on the right reverses E left-to-right, giving **Ǝ**. (A)

**Q10.** 3 small triangles + pairs (1+2, 2+3) + whole (1+2+3) = 3 + 2 + 1 = **6**. (C)

**Q11.** P + Q means P is mother of Q; Q − R means Q is brother of R; so **P is mother of Q and Q is brother of R**. (C)

**Q12.** Folding once and cutting the corner on the fold line yields a single hole when unfolded = **1**. (A)

**Q13.** 121=11², 144=12², 169=13², 196=14²; **180** is not a perfect square. (C)

**Q14.** G=7, R=18, A=1, P=16, E=5 → **7-18-1-16-5**. (A)

**Q15.** Order: Esha, Anil, Bina, Chetan, Dev. Middle position is **Bina**. (B)

**Q16.** −15 − 8 + 20 = −23 + 20 = **−3**. (A)

**Q17.** (3/4) × (16/9) = 48/36 = **4/3**. (A)

**Q18.** 0 lies between −1/2 and 1/2. (B)

**Q19.** 3x = 18 → x = **6**. (C)

**Q20.** 90° − 35° = **55°**. (B)

**Q21.** 180° − (48° + 72°) = 180° − 120° = **60°**. (B)

**Q22.** Two sides and included angle = **SAS**. (C)

**Q23.** 15% of 800 = 0.15 × 800 = **₹120**. (B)

**Q24.** (5a − 2a) + (3b + 4b) = **3a + 7b**. (A)

**Q25.** 2³ × 2⁴ = 2^(3+4) = **2⁷**. (A)

**Q26.** Side = 48/4 = 12 cm; area = 12² = **144 cm²**. (B)

**Q27.** Sum = 90; mean = 90/5 = **18**. (C)

**Q28.** A regular hexagon has **6** lines of symmetry. (C)

**Q29.** A cuboid has **6** faces. (B)

**Q30.** 0.375 = 375/1000 = **3/8**. (A)

**Q31.** Girls = 18 × (2/3) = **12**. (B)

**Q32.** SI = (5000 × 8 × 2)/100 = **₹800**. (B)

**Q33.** Area = ½ × 10 × 6 = **30 cm²**. (B)

**Q34.** 5⁰ + 5¹ = 1 + 5 = **6**. (B)

**Q35.** Total 180°; larger = (5/9) × 180 = **100°**. (C)

**Q36.** Profit = 4620 − 4200 = 420; % = (420/4200) × 100 = **10%**. (B)

**Q37.** Total cost = 3×40 + 2×50 = 120 + 100 = 220; per kg = 220/5 = **₹44**. (B)

**Q38.** Read = 300×(2/5) + 300×(1/3) = 120 + 100 = 220; left = 300 − 220 = **80**. (B)

**Q39.** 240 ÷ 8 = **30 minutes**. (B)

**Q40.** Discount = 20% of 1200 = 240; SP = 1200 − 240 = **₹960**. (B)

**Q41.** Monthly saving = 15% of 24000 = 3600; yearly = 3600 × 12 = **₹43200**. (B)

**Q42.** Speed = 180/3 = 60 km/h; distance = 60 × 5 = **300 km**. (C)

**Q43.** Perimeter = 2(25 + 18) = 2 × 43 = **86 m**. (B)

**Q44.** 36 ÷ 4 = 9 each; each gives back 2 → keeps 9 − 2 = **7**. (B)

**Q45.** −4 + 11 = **7°C**. (C)

**Q46.** Let number = n: n/3 + 8 = n/2 → 8 = n/2 − n/3 = n/6 → n = **48**. (C)

**Q47.** Difference between 5% gain and 10% loss = 15% of CP = ₹90 → CP = 90/0.15 = **₹600**. (C)

**Q48.** Let B = x: A = 2x, C = x + 20. Sum: 2x + x + (x + 20) = 180 → 4x = 160 → x = 40; so A = 2×40 = **80°**. (C)

**Q49.** Numbers averaging 20: 16, 18, 20, 22, 24. Product of smallest and largest = 16 × 24 = **384**. (C)

**Q50.** Difference (5/8 − 3/8) = 2/8 = 1/4 of capacity = 15 L → capacity = 15 × 4 = **60 litres**. (C)
