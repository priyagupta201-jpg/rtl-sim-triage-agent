## Detailed Solutions

**Q1.** Differences are 3, 5, 7, 9, so next difference is 11: 27 + 11 = 38. **(B)**

**Q2.** Each letter shifts forward by 1: L→M, A→B, M→N, P→Q. So DESK → E, F, T, L. **(A)**

**Q3.** We hear with the ear; we taste with the tongue. **(C)**

**Q4.** 7 × 7 = 49, so 11 × 11 = 121. **(B)**

**Q5.** "My grandfather's only son" is the man's father; his daughter is the man's sister. **(A)**

**Q6.** He faces North, turns right (East, walks 8 m), turns right again (now facing South). **(B)**

**Q7.** A triangle with two cevians from two vertices crossing inside creates 8 triangles in total (small and combined). **(C)**

**Q8.** 24, 36, 64 are related in a set pattern (24 = 4!, 36 = 6², 64 = 8²... ) — the intended odd one is 42, which is neither a perfect square nor fits the pattern of the others. **(C)**

**Q9.** C = 3, A = 1, T = 20; sum = 3 + 1 + 20 = 24. **(C)**

**Q10.** In a vertical mirror image, the order of the letters reverses and each letter is flipped left-to-right (laterally inverted). **(A)**

**Q11.** Position from right = (Total − position from left) + 1 = (30 − 12) + 1 = 19th. **(C)**

**Q12.** In each row, output = first number × 6: 5 × 6 = 30. (Also equals sum of the three numbers.) **(C)**

**Q13.** Replace symbols: 6 × 2 ÷ 4 + 3 = (12 ÷ 4) + 3 = 3 + 3 = 6. **(D)**

**Q14.** First letters go A, B, C, D → E; second letters go Z, Y, X, W → V. So EV. **(A)**

**Q15.** Order from left: R, Q, P, S, T. The middle (3rd) seat is P. **(A)**

**Q16.** Heterotrophic nutrition means taking in ready-made food made by others. **(B)**

**Q17.** Stomata are the tiny pores on leaves for gas exchange. **(A)**

**Q18.** Cuscuta (Amarbel) is a parasitic plant with no chlorophyll that draws food from a host. **(B)**

**Q19.** Chlorophyll is the green pigment that traps sunlight for photosynthesis. **(B)**

**Q20.** Ruminants like cows bring food back to the mouth to chew again; this is rumination. **(A)**

**Q21.** Normal human body temperature is about 37 °C (98.6 °F). **(B)**

**Q22.** The kink stops mercury from flowing back, so the reading can be taken after removal. **(B)**

**Q23.** In solids, heat travels by conduction (particle to particle). **(C)**

**Q24.** Wool traps air; trapped air is a poor conductor, preventing body-heat loss. **(B)**

**Q25.** Turmeric turns red in a basic solution (and stays yellow in acidic/neutral). **(B)**

**Q26.** Acid + base → salt + water (neutralisation). **(B)**

**Q27.** Ant and nettle stings contain formic acid, neutralised by baking soda (a mild base). **(B)**

**Q28.** Rusting forms a new substance (iron oxide), so it is a chemical change. **(B)**

**Q29.** Rusting needs both air (oxygen) and moisture (water). **(C)**

**Q30.** Galvanisation coats iron with a layer of zinc to prevent rusting. **(B)**

**Q31.** Polar bears have a thick fat layer for insulation and white fur for warmth and camouflage. **(B)**

**Q32.** Rainforests are warm, humid and have plenty of food, allowing rich biodiversity. **(C)**

**Q33.** A cyclone is a violent tropical storm rotating around a low-pressure centre. **(B)**

**Q34.** Warm air is lighter (less dense) than cold air, so it rises. **(C)**

**Q35.** Topsoil is the dark, humus-rich upper layer best for plant growth. **(C)**

**Q36.** Sandy soil has large particles and big air spaces, so water percolates fastest. **(C)**

**Q37.** Respiration releases carbon dioxide. **(C)**

**Q38.** Fish exchange gases through their gills. **(B)**

**Q39.** Anaerobic respiration in yeast gives alcohol + CO₂ + a small amount of energy. **(B)**

**Q40.** The rhythmic throb felt in an artery is the pulse. **(A)**

**Q41.** Xylem carries water and minerals upward from roots to leaves. **(B)**

**Q42.** Pollination is the transfer of pollen from anther to stigma. **(B)**

**Q43.** Constant (uniform) speed means equal distances in equal time intervals. **(B)**

**Q44.** An electric iron works on the heating effect of electric current. **(B)**

**Q45.** A plane mirror forms a virtual, erect image of the same size as the object. **(B)**

**Q46.** Time for first 30 km = 30/60 = 0.5 h; time for next 30 km = 30/30 = 1 h. Total time = 1.5 h for 60 km. Average speed = 60 / 1.5 = 40 km/h. (Note: it is NOT the simple average of 45.) **(B)**

**Q47.** Acids turn blue litmus red (W and Z do this → acidic). Bases turn red litmus blue (X does this → basic). Y changes neither → neutral. **(A)**

**Q48.** In a series circuit there is a single path; a broken filament breaks the whole circuit, so the other bulb also goes off. **(B)**

**Q49.** Souring of milk (i) and burning of a candle (iii) form new substances → chemical changes. Evaporation (ii) and cutting paper (iv) are physical changes. **(A)**

**Q50.** The land heats faster, warming the air above it; this warm air rises and cooler sea air moves in to replace it, forming the sea breeze. **(B)**
