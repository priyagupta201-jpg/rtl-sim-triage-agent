## Detailed Solutions

**Q1.** Differences: 4, 9, 16, 25 (i.e. 2², 3², 4², 5²). Next difference = 6² = 36, so 57 + 36 = **93**. → (B)

**Q2.** Each letter shifts +1: M→N, O→P, N→O, D→E, A→B, Y→Z. So FRIDAY → F+1=G, R+1=S, I+1=J, D+1=E, A+1=B, Y+1=Z = **GSJEBZ**. → (A)

**Q3.** A square has 4 sides; a hexagon has **6** sides. → (B)

**Q4.** "The only son of my grandmother" = Rohan's father. His daughter = Rohan's **sister**. → (A)

**Q5.** Face South → Right turn faces West → Right turn faces North → Left turn faces West. Final facing = **West**. → (D)

**Q6.** Position from right = (Total + 1) − position from left = (40 + 1) − 11 = **30th**. → (B)

**Q7.** Rule: third = (first)² + second. Row 1: 4² + 9 = 25 ✓. Row 2: 5² + 11 = 36 ✓. Row 3: 6² + 13 = **49**. → (C)

**Q8.** Each letter +1: A→B, C→D, E→F, G→H gives BDFH. So PRTV → Q, S, U, W = **QSUW**. → (A)

**Q9.** With a horizontal midline and a median from apex to base, the standard count gives **8** triangles (small top, subdivisions, and combined larger triangles). → (D)

**Q10.** Replace: 16 ÷ 4 × 3 + 8 − 2 = 4 × 3 + 8 − 2 = 12 + 8 − 2 = **18**. → (A)

**Q11.** Folding twice creates 4 layers; one punch through all layers gives **4** holes when unfolded. → (D)

**Q12.** A vertical mirror reverses left–right: the word order reverses and each letter is laterally inverted. → (C)

**Q13.** Deepak > Ajay > Ravi > Sunil > Mohan. Tallest = **Deepak**. → (C)

**Q14.** 8=2³, 27=3³, 64=4³, 125=5³ are perfect cubes; **100** is not a perfect cube. → (C)

**Q15.** Letters decrease by 3: Z(26), W(23), T(20), Q(17), next = 14 = **N**. → (A)

**Q16.** (−15) + 24 + 8 − 17 = 9 + 8 − 17 = 17 − 17 = **0**. → (A)

**Q17.** (−3/4) × (8/9) = −24/36 = −2/3. Then (−2/3) ÷ (2/3) = (−2/3) × (3/2) = **−1**. → (A)

**Q18.** All are negative. Comparing magnitudes: −7/12 ≈ −0.583, −5/8 = −0.625, −2/3 ≈ −0.667, −3/4 = −0.75. Largest (closest to 0) = **−7/12**. → (D)

**Q19.** 3x − 6 + 5 = 2x + 7 → 3x − 1 = 2x + 7 → x = **8**. → (B)

**Q20.** Vertically opposite angle = 55°; adjacent (linear pair) = 180 − 55 = **125°**. → (A)

**Q21.** Two angles sum = 180 − 80 = 100°, in ratio 2:3 → 40° and 60°. Smallest of all three = **40°**. → (B)

**Q22.** Two angles and the included side → **ASA**. → (C)

**Q23.** Discount = 15% of 800 = 120. SP = 800 − 120 = **₹680**. → (A)

**Q24.** x-terms: 5x + 2x − 4x = 3x. y-terms: −3y + 7y = 4y. → **3x + 4y**. → (A)

**Q25.** 2^(5−3+0) = 2² = **4**. → (B)

**Q26.** 3,45,000 = 3.45 × 10⁵. → (C)

**Q27.** Width = (60/2) − 18 = 30 − 18 = 12. Area = 18 × 12 = **216 cm²**. → (A)

**Q28.** Area = ½ × 14 × 9 = **63 cm²**. → (A)

**Q29.** First five multiples of 6: 6,12,18,24,30. Sum = 90, mean = 90/5 = **18**. → (B)

**Q30.** 8 appears three times (most frequent). Mode = **8**. → (B)

**Q31.** A regular pentagon has **5** lines of symmetry. → (C)

**Q32.** A cuboid: **12 edges, 6 faces, 8 vertices**. → (A)

**Q33.** 0.6³ = 0.216. → (A)

**Q34.** (2/5)·n = 30 → n = 75. (3/4)·75 = **56.25**. → (C)

**Q35.** Let angle = x. Complement: 90 − x = 3x → 90 = 4x → x = **22.5°**. → (A)

**Q36.** Apples 3×120 = 360; grapes 2×90 = 180; total 540. Change = 1000 − 540 = **₹460**. → (A)

**Q37.** Speed = 240/3 = 80 km/h. In 5 h = 80 × 5 = **400 km**. → (B)

**Q38.** SI = (8000 × 5 × 2)/100 = **₹800**. → (B)

**Q39.** Empty part = 1/3 of 450 = **150 L**. → (A)

**Q40.** Profit = 2900 − 2500 = 400. Profit% = 400/2500 × 100 = **16%**. → (C)

**Q41.** 630 ÷ 45 = **14 days**. → (C)

**Q42.** Perimeter = 2(25+16) = 82 m. Cost = 82 × 35 = **₹2,870**. → (A)

**Q43.** Girls = 60% of 40 = 24, so boys = 40 − 24 = **16**. → (B)

**Q44.** Flour 5 parts = 350 g → 1 part = 70 g. Sugar 2 parts = **140 g**. → (B)

**Q45.** Savings = 25% of 24000 = 6000/month. Yearly = 6000 × 12 = **₹72,000**. → (C)

**Q46.** MP = 800 × 1.40 = 1120. SP = 1120 × 0.75 = 840. Profit = 840 − 800 = 40. Profit% = 40/800 × 100 = **5% profit**. → (A)

**Q47.** Sum: 2x + (3x−10) + (x+10) = 180 → 6x = 180 → x = 30. Then P = 60°, Q = 3(30)−10 = 80°, R = 40°. Largest = angle Q = **80°**. → (B)

**Q48.** Other = −4/9 − 2/3 = −4/9 − 6/9 = −10/9. Reciprocal of −10/9 = **−9/10**. → (A)

**Q49.** Outer rectangle (path adds 5 m on every side): (90+10) × (60+10) = 100 × 70 = 7000 m². Field = 90 × 60 = 5400 m². Path area = 7000 − 5400 = **1,600 m²**. → (A)

**Q50.** (2³)² = 2⁶ = 64; numerator = 64 × 81 = 5184. Denominator = 16 × 9 = 144. 5184 ÷ 144 = **36**. → (A)
